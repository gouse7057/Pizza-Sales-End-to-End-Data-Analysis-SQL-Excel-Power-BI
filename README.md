# Pizza-Sales-End-to-End-Data-Analysis-SQL-Excel-Power-BI
The Pizza Sales Analysis is project designed to analyse transactional sales data from a pizza store. The goal is to identify key business insights, trends and KPIs that will help management make informed decisions related to sales, marketing and operations.

🎯 Business Objectives
• Identify total revenue, total pizzas sold, and total orders
• Analyze sales distribution by category, size, and product type
•	Examine time-based sales trends (daily, monthly, hourly)
•	Identify best-selling and worst-selling pizzas
•	Understand customer purchasing behavior using AOV and order patterns
•	Build interactive dashboards for monitoring KPIs

❓Business Question Answered
•	What is total revenue generated?
•	How many pizzas were sold in equal?
•	How category and size of pizzas perform best?
•	Which pizzas are the top and bottom performers?
•	What is the average order value and average pizza per order?
•	What are the sales trends by day, month and time of the day?

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
• Removed missing and inconsistent records
• Standardized date and time formats
• Created calculated fields (revenue, AOV, KPIs)
• Structured data for dashboard reporting

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
• SQL (MySQL) — Data extraction, transformation, joins, aggregations, and business query analysis
• Microsoft Excel — Data preprocessing, pivot table analysis, KPI calculations, and dashboard creation
• Power BI — Interactive business intelligence dashboards and performance monitoring
• Data Analysis & Business Intelligence — Trend analysis, KPI tracking, and decision-support insights

📈 Key Insights

• Weekend evenings generate the highest sales volume
• Large size pizzas contribute the highest revenue share
• Classic category dominates total orders
• Certain menu items consistently underperform and impact profitability
• Sales peak between 12 PM – 1 PM and 4 PM – 8 PM

📌 Conclusion & Recommendations
The analysis provides a comprehensive view of pizza sales performance, Management can leverage these insights to:
•	Focus marketing on high-performance categories.
•	Optimize the menu by reconsidering least selling pizzas.
•	Plan inventory and staffing based on sales peaks.
•	Monitor KPIs regularly through dashboards for continuous improvement
